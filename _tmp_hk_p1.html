    • Indoor LED Displays - Vision meets innovation

    • Supported by SMD, IMD, and COB encapsulation technologies, Hikvision’s Indoor LED Displays provide state-of-the-art solutions for any indoor scenario. These indoor LED screen boasts superior image quality, high resolution, and dynamic color reproduction, giving customers and visitors an indelible visual experience, and enabling users to conveniently operate through Hikvision’s patented, image-improving technology and remote control functionality.

    • Indoor LED Displays - Vision meets innovation

    • Supported by SMD, IMD, and COB encapsulation technologies, Hikvision’s Indoor LED Displays provide state-of-the-art solutions for any indoor scenario. These indoor LED screen boasts superior image quality, high resolution, and dynamic color reproduction, giving customers and visitors an indelible visual experience, and enabling users to conveniently operate through Hikvision’s patented, image-improving technology and remote control functionality.

    Solid Series

    · Ultra-light & ultra-thin (less than 30mm)

    · Equipped with high-quality lamps for enhanced protection

    · Flip-chip & common cathode (optional)

    Ultra Series

     · Ultra-light & ultra-thin (less than 30mm)

     · Single-cabinet correction for easy installation

     · Energy-saving up to 50% with high-performance IC and technology

     · COB design, anti-glare, high-contrast, and high-protection

    Flex II Series

     · Creative splicing with diverse sizes, suitable for retail scenarios

     · 90° rotation to ensure optimal viewing

     · Factory standard with 45 degrees cut edge, can directly meet the  · External right angle creative design

    Value Series

    · Module cable-free design for easier installation and front maintenance

    · Ultra-thin back shell for excellent flatness

    · Ultra-high contrast ratio for excellent color performance

    Prime Series

     · Precision bin-mixing for consistent color performance

     · Flip-chip and common-cathode design for lower power consumption and heat

     · Epoxy resin encapsulation for enhanced moisture and dust protection
